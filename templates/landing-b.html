The loop

  1. 01

    Material goes in

    Files and pasted text are chunked and sorted into topics.

  2. 02

    Understanding is scored

    Quizzes and chat move each topic's score up or down.

  3. 03

    The roadmap reorders

    Weak topics rise; your goal's focus follows them.

  4. 04

    Αθηνα checks back

    Scheduled runs revisit the gaps and log what they found.

What it does

Six places to work, one shared picture of what you know.

  • Goals

    Say what you want to learn and Αθηνα drafts a roadmap for it — milestones in the order they should be taken, each tied to a topic. Every goal carries a percent done and a current focus.

    Open Goals →

  • Materials

    Upload slides, notes and readings, or paste text straight in. Each file is split into chunks, tagged into topics, and given an understanding score so you can see where you actually stand.

    Open Materials →

  • Sessions

    Chat with Αθηνα about anything in your material. It answers from your own chunks rather than from thin air, and what it notices in the conversation feeds your topic scores.

    Open Sessions →

  • Quizzes

    Quizzes are generated from a topic you have material for. Answers are graded against the source chunks, and the result is recorded as evidence behind that topic's score.

    Open Quizzes →

  • Knowledge-Sync

    The log of what Αθηνα did without being asked — scheduled runs across your material and goals, and the actions it took off the back of them.

    Open Knowledge-Sync →

  • Dashboard

    The one screen for today: goals still in progress, the last check-in Αθηνα made, and the weak topics that are shaping what it thinks you should work on.

    Open Dashboard →

“Every topic, quiz question and roadmap step comes from material you uploaded yourself.” So the score Αθηνα keeps is a score of your syllabus — not someone else's.
