Materials

Controls how Hermes builds topic confidence scores from your files and chats.

Allow LLM-auto embedding

New uploads are automatically chunked, tagged to a topic, and embedded without you reviewing the tagging first.

Chat sessions as a confidence signal

Powers Materials

Lets how you talk through a topic in chat — hesitation, re-asking, confident explanation — affect that topic's confidence score, not just quiz results.

Auto re-quiz on decay

Hermes re-checks a topic you haven't touched in a while, in case confidence has quietly dropped.

Goal

Controls how much Hermes can act on your roadmap without checking with you first.

Allow external sources

If a milestone can't be grounded in your uploaded Materials, let Hermes research it externally instead of leaving it as a guess.

Milestone auto-reorder without approval

When off, Hermes proposes a reorder and waits for your confirmation. When on, it reorders your roadmap immediately when new signal comes in.
